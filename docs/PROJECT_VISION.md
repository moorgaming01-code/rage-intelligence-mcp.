# RAGE Intelligence MCP
## Project Vision

### 1. Mission

Build an independent, open-source research and knowledge platform for studying Rockstar's RAGE technology and publicly documented or observable GTA VI systems.

Our goal is to organise technical knowledge, investigate engine systems, establish relationships between subsystems, and eventually expose this knowledge through a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server.

### 2. Core Objectives

- Research Rockstar's RAGE technology.
- Study publicly documented GTA VI technologies.
- Build a structured technical knowledge base.
- Map relationships between engine subsystems.
- Develop evidence-based technical models.
- Create an MCP server for AI-assisted research.
- Support community contributions and independent investigation.

### 3. Research Subsystems

Our initial research areas are:

1. Engine Architecture
2. World and Asset Streaming
3. Memory and Resource Management
4. CPU Scheduling and Parallel Jobs
5. Graphics and Rendering
6. Physics and Vehicle Simulation
7. Animation and Character Systems
8. NPC and Gameplay AI
9. Audio Systems
10. Platform and Hardware Integration

### 4. Evidence Standards

Every technical claim must be classified as one of:

- Confirmed
- Reported
- Inference
- Hypothesis
- Unknown

We will never present speculation as confirmed Rockstar technology.

### 5. MCP Development

The future MCP server will provide AI assistants with structured access to our research.

Initial capabilities will include knowledge searching, subsystem lookup, evidence retrieval and research navigation.

### 6. Project Principles

- Open-source collaboration.
- Technical accuracy.
- Transparent research.
- Respect for intellectual property.
- Public and authorised information only.
- Knowledge sharing and education.

### 7. Long-Term Vision

Build a comprehensive, searchable engineering research platform that helps developers, researchers and enthusiasts better understand RAGE technology and publicly documented GTA VI systems.

**Research first. Evidence always. Engineering together.**
