## Research Rules

Every subsystem must eventually contain:

1. Research documentation.
2. Verified source references.
3. Evidence records.
4. Technical hypotheses.
5. Known limitations.
6. Open questions.
7. Dependency relationships.

No undocumented implementation detail should be presented as confirmed fact.

---

## Long-Term Objective

Build an interconnected technical knowledge base capable of helping researchers understand the relationships between RAGE-related technologies and publicly documented GTA VI systems.
