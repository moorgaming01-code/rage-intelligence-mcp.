## Research Rules

Every question must eventually have:

- A documented answer or an explicit unresolved status.
- Supporting sources.
- Evidence classification.
- Confidence assessment.
- Research notes.
- Last-reviewed date.

Never turn an assumption into an established fact.

---

## Long-Term Goal

Build a comprehensive, evidence-based understanding of publicly documented RAGE technology and GTA VI systems.

An unanswered question is not a failure.

It is the beginning of an investigation.
